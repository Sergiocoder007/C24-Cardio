Support

If something in C24 Cardio is not working, or you have a question about the timer, history, or this website, you can reach us through the public project repository.

HOW TO GET HELP

Open a GitHub issue with a short description of what happened, the device or browser you used, and the steps that led to the problem. That is the current support channel for C24 Cardio.

github.com/Sergiocoder007/C24-Cardio/issues

You can also view the project here: github.com/Sergiocoder007/C24-Cardio

BEFORE YOU WRITE

These details help us understand the issue faster:

  • whether you were using the website or the iOS app
  • the rounds, work time, and rest time you selected
  • whether sound was on
  • what you expected, and what you saw instead

PRIVACY QUESTIONS

C24 Cardio stores workout history on your device and does not use a C24 Cardio backend for that data. Read the Privacy Policy for the current data practices.
