Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how C24 Cardio (“C24Cardio”, “we”, “us”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the C24 Cardio website at https://c24cardio.fit and the C24 Cardio Android application (package name com.c24cardio.app).

WHO WE ARE

C24 Cardio is a combat-conditioning round timer. You can use it in a web browser or as an Android app. This policy applies to both.

The codebase and public website do not list a separate legal company name or email address. For privacy questions, contact us through the website: https://c24cardio.fit.

WHAT THIS APP DOES

C24 Cardio lets you choose round count, work duration, and rest duration, search a local list of fighter names, run a workout timer, play timer sounds on your device, and reveal fighter artwork as work time is completed. Workout settings and timer progress exist only while the page or app session is open.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

C24 Cardio does not create user accounts and does not ask you to sign in. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, or other profile information.

Based on the current implementation, we do not operate a C24 Cardio backend that stores:

  • account or login data
  • workout history or timer results
  • fighter search queries
  • analytics events
  • advertising identifiers
  • cookies or local storage written by our own code

Round settings, selected fighter, sound on/off, and timer state are kept in the app’s memory for the current session only. They are not saved by our code to disk, cookies, or a server. Closing or refreshing the app discards that session.

INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

  • No user accounts or authentication
  • No email, password, or profile forms
  • No in-app purchases or payment processing in the current code
  • No push notifications (Firebase / Google services config is not included)
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the app source
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions requested by the Android manifest

HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Session data is used only on your device to run the timer, play sounds you enable, and show workout progress and artwork. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or marketing lists.

HOW INFORMATION IS STORED AND PROTECTED

Because our code does not send workout or account data to a C24 Cardio server, we do not maintain a user database to protect. Sounds used by the timer are generated or loaded from files bundled with the app. Your device and browser still apply their own security controls.

The Android app allows standard Android backup of application data (android:allowBackup="true"). Our application code does not write workout or profile records to storage. If the operating system backs up any WebView data, that is handled by Android, not by a C24 Cardio account system.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES ACTUALLY USED

The current product uses the following third parties. We do not invent additional services.

  • Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org): most fighter portraits are loaded from publicly hosted Wikipedia/Wikimedia image URLs when you view a fighter. Wikimedia may receive a normal web request from your device (for example IP address and browser/app details) according to Wikimedia’s own policies.
  • Amazon CloudFront (UFC-hosted image) (dmxg5wxfqgb4y.cloudfront.net): one fighter image is loaded from this CDN. The CDN operator may log the request as part of ordinary image delivery.
  • Website and app hosting / distribution: visiting c24cardio.fit or installing the Android app from Google Play means the host or store may process technical data (such as IP address) under their own terms. C24 Cardio source code does not add a separate analytics SDK for this.
  • Capacitor / Android system WebView: the Android app displays the same web app inside a WebView. Capacitor is used as the wrapper. No extra Capacitor analytics, push, or ads plugins are present in the current project.

A Google services Gradle plugin exists in the Android build files only as an optional hook. There is no google-services.json in the project, so Firebase Cloud Messaging and similar Google mobile services are not enabled.

ANDROID PERMISSIONS

The Android app currently requests INTERNET so the WebView can load the app and so fighter images can be fetched from the hosts listed above. The manifest does not request camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or photos access.

COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE

C24 Cardio application code does not set cookies and does not write to localStorage or sessionStorage. Your browser or WebView may still keep its own technical data. Third-party image hosts may set cookies if their servers are configured to do so; that is outside C24 Cardio’s code.

SHARING WITH THIRD PARTIES

We do not sell personal information. We do not share workout or account data with third parties because we do not collect that data on a C24 Cardio server. Image requests described above go directly from your device to those image hosts.

DATA RETENTION

Session workout settings are not retained by C24 Cardio after you leave the session. We do not keep a server-side copy of your workouts. Third-party image hosts retain their own server logs according to their policies.

DATA DELETION

Because we do not store user accounts or workout history on a C24 Cardio server, there is no C24 Cardio profile to delete. Clearing the website or uninstalling the Android app removes the app from your device. If you believe a third-party image host has logged a request, you would need to use that host’s own process.

YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal data. For C24 Cardio, we do not hold an account record we can look up. Contact us at https://c24cardio.fit if you have a privacy request or question.

CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

C24 Cardio is a general fitness timer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not provide a sign-up flow that would collect a child’s email or identity.

CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

If the app later adds accounts, analytics, ads, or other collection, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to that use.
